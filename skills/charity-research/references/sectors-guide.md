# Wayfinder Sector Guide

## Sector Categories

Wayfinder classifies charities using a two-level taxonomy: Sector → Sub-sector.

### Major Sectors (by charity count)

| Sector | Typical Income Range | Key Sub-sectors |
|--------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Education & Training | £10K–£500M | Schools, Universities, Vocational, Educational Research |
| Health & Medical | £50K–£1B | Medical Research, Hospitals, Patient Support |
| Children & Young People | £10K–£200M | Youth Clubs, Children's Activities, Child Protection |
| Community Development | £5K–£100M | Village Halls, Community Centres, Local Development |
| International Development | £100K–£2B | Global Health, Disaster Relief, Development Aid |
| Arts Culture & Heritage | £10K–£500M | Museums, Theatres, Heritage Conservation |
| Religion | £5K–£100M | Churches, Faith Groups, Religious Education |

| Hospice & End of Life | £1M–£50M | Adult Hospices, Children's Hospices |
| Housing & Homelessness | £100K–£200M | Homeless Shelters, Social Housing |

### Grantmaker Identification

Grantmakers (charities that fund other charities) can be identified by:

1. **Sector**: Often classified as "General Charitable Purposes" or "Philanthropy"
2. **Activities description**: Contains words like "grants", "funding", "grantmaking"
3. **Financial pattern**: High income relative to expenditure (building endowment) or expenditure dominated by grants made
4. **Name patterns**: Often include "Foundation", "Trust", "Fund", "Endowment"

### Financial Analysis Tips

When analysing charity finances:

- **Income vs Expenditure ratio**: Healthy charities typically spend 80-100% of income. Consistently spending >100% may indicate unsustainability.
- **Year-over-year trends**: Look for consistent growth vs decline. Sharp changes often indicate major events (mergers, grant cycles, COVID impact).
- **Size brackets**: Micro (<£10K), Small (£10K–£100K), Medium (£100K–£1M), Large (£1M–£10M), Major (£10M+), Super-major (£100M+).
- **Filing delays**: Some charities file late — check `latestAccountsDate` to see how current the data is.
